Achievement Hunter for Steam · Browser extension

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The short version

  • Achievement Hunter keeps everything in your own browser. Nothing about you or your Steam account is sent to us.
  • It only talks to Steam: the achievement pages you open, your own games page, and your friends’ public Steam pages to compare with them.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers. We never sell or share anything.
  • If you buy Pro, ExtensionPay and Stripe handle the payment. We never see your card details.

What Achievement Hunter for Steam is

Achievement Hunter for Steam is a browser extension made by Black Ribbon Studios. It adds rarity tiers, filters, guide links, a hunt list and a “My games” overview to Steam Community achievement pages. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Valve. Steam is a trademark of Valve Corporation.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your browsing history, your Steam login, your Steam ID or the content of any page.
  • We don't use analytics, trackers or advertising in the extension.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone.

What stays on your device

The extension saves these in your browser's extension storage, on your own computer:

  • Your settings, such as your default sort. If you've turned on sync in your browser, your browser may sync these between your own devices.
  • Your hunt list: the achievements you pin.
  • “My games”: your games, their achievement lists and your progress, read from your own Steam pages.
  • A temporary copy of public achievement data from Steam, so pages load faster.

You can remove games and pins on the extension's Hunt list and My games pages, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

Payments

Pro is a one-time purchase handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. We never see your card details. ExtensionPay tells the extension whether you've paid and which email address you used, so you can restore Pro in another browser. Their own privacy policies cover the payment itself.

Your choices

Because your data stays in your browser, you're in control of it: delete games or pins in the extension, or uninstall it to remove everything. For anything about a Pro purchase, such as a refund or the email you paid with, write to us and we'll help.

Changes

If the extension starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
